VERDICT
Structure follows the classical ORB-SLAM-style pipeline, but several parts are
simplified in ways that will hurt real-world tracking. The 54 tests use
synthetic dot grids and mostly check counts, so they won't catch the problems
below.

LIKELY BUGS (fix first)
- Map culling breaks keyframe links: cull_map_points compacts g_map, but
  keyframes store indices (map_point_ids). After the first cull, IDs point at
  the wrong points. Use stable IDs or a free-list.
- Duplicate landmarks: every new keyframe re-triangulates all matches instead
  of associating with existing map points. The map fills with duplicates and
  the O(n^2) dedup in match_against_local_map has to compensate.
- IMU frame mismatch: slam_process_frame uses raw gravity, while
  slam_get_floor_plane flips y/z. Overwriting R's up-axis also changes the
  camera center (C = -R^T t) because t isn't corrected. Raw
  accelerationIncludingGravity is noisy, and iOS sign conventions differ
  from Android.
- Hull buffer overflow risk: out_hull has no size parameter, so a large hull
  overruns the caller's buffer.
- Unscaled thresholds: translation is normalized to 1.0, so the 0.10/0.25
  distance thresholds and the 0.05 plane tolerance are in arbitrary units,
  not cm, and are not rescaled after calibration.
- Shared globals, no locking: Vision and BA workers would race on g_map.

GAPS VS. INDUSTRY STANDARD
ORB
- No image pyramid, so no scale invariance.
- Pattern comes from an LCG instead of OpenCV's learned pairs; no
  pre-smoothing.
- Rotated offsets are truncated, not rounded.
- +/-15 pattern rotates out to ~21 px, past the 16 px margin.
- FAST score is a contiguous count, not a corner score.

Initialization
- Mostly planar floor makes the 8-point essential matrix degenerate.
  Standard: homography vs. fundamental model selection plus a parallax check.
- The computed F is never used.

RANSAC
- Sampson threshold 0.001 and PnP threshold 0.001 are ~15-17 px at 60 deg
  FOV. Typical values are 1-4 px.
- Fixed iteration counts, not adaptive.

PnP
- 6-point DLT inside RANSAC; standard is P3P/EPnP.
- Gauss-Newton refinement runs on all points, outliers included, no LM
  damping.

Tracking
- Descriptor matching only: no motion model, no projection-based guided
  search.
- No keyframe gating while tracking is lost, so a bad pose can insert
  keyframes.

"BA"
- Actually single-keyframe motion-only GN plus a heuristic depth nudge.
- Real local BA jointly optimizes a keyframe window and points using LM, a
  Huber kernel, and the Schur complement.
- Runs synchronously, not in the worker REQ 3.3 requires.

Triangulation
- Normal equations with no reprojection or parallax checks.

Concave hull
- Heuristic edge-digging, not a real alpha shape. Use Delaunay with
  circumradius filtering.
- Cost is roughly O(h*n^2) per iteration.

SVD
- Eigendecomposition of A^T A squares the condition number, despite the
  comment. Use one-sided Jacobi or Golub-Kahan.

Build
- --export-all leaks internals.
- <iostream> and always-on DEBUG are risky under -fno-exceptions and in Wasm.
- No -msimd128, though Safari 16.4+ supports it.

WHAT'S SOLID
- Strict FAST-9 contiguity check and intensity-centroid orientation.
- Ratio test, cheirality check, Hartley normalization.
- Double-precision accumulation in eigensolvers; NaN and degenerate-input
  guards.
- Gravity-gated plane RANSAC that rejects before counting inliers.

TEST QUALITY
- Several asserts are tautologies (assert(true), size() >= 0).
- Asserts vanish under NDEBUG.
- No test uses real data (TUM RGB-D, EuRoC) with ATE/RPE metrics.
